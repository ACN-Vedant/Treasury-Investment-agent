A fresh workbook upload is required for every run. On upload, the system automatically runs surplus analysis, credit ratings assessment and compliance checks, then presents investment recommendations for your approval. Expected sheets: Cash Balances, Loan Schedule, Investment Positions. Market Rates & Policy is no longer uploaded — the daily rate fields are fetched automatically (see status below).
Enter the haircut % to be considered as buffer from the net surplus balance available
How it works: On upload, each of the 3 sheets is parsed and passed to the agents. Market rates are sourced live and merged in automatically. The Reporting Agent adds maturing investment principal and interest receivable (from the Investment Positions sheet) and deducts upcoming principal repayments and interest payables (from the Loan Schedule sheet) within the investment horizon from each account's gross surplus, then applies your margin haircut before recommending investable amounts.
